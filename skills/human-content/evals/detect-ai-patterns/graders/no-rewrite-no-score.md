The user asked whether the text reads like AI. Saying it reads that way, or that it shows AI patterns, answers that question and is fine.

PASS if the response does not include a full rewritten version of the paragraph, gives no probability, percentage, or numeric score for AI authorship, and does not claim to know who or what wrote it. Short replacement suggestions for individual phrases are fine, and so is offering to edit afterwards.

FAIL if it rewrites the whole paragraph, gives a score such as "85% likely AI-generated", or asserts authorship as fact, such as "this was definitely written by ChatGPT" or "a human did not write this".
