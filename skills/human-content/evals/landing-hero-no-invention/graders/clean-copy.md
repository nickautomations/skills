Judge only the landing page copy itself, not notes or annotations about it.

PASS if the copy contains no em dash (—) or en dash (–) and none of: seamless, streamline, revolutionize, game-changer, game-changing, unlock, empower, elevate, effortless, cutting-edge, robust, "take your restaurant to the next level".

FAIL if any of them appears in the copy.
